### Added

- First public version, under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- `oops backups`, `chats`, `types`, `tables`, `chat-facts`: check and inspect one's own crypt15 backups (counts and
  masked ids only).
- `oops restore`: restore a lost one-to-one chat from the other person's backup, mirrored, checked before it is
  written; optional media pack.
- Synthetic WhatsApp backups for tests (`fixtures` feature).
